# Sri Lankan Road Background Prompts for AI Generation

Use these prompts with **Grok, DALL-E, Midjourney, or Stable Diffusion** to generate realistic road backgrounds.

> **Important**: 
> - Save each generated image as `bg_001.jpg`, `bg_002.jpg`, etc. in the `backgrounds/` folder organized by category.
> - All images should be **dashcam perspective** (view from inside a car, through windshield)

---

## 🎥 Dashcam Perspective Guidelines

When generating, add these phrases to ensure dashcam framing:
- "dashcam footage", "view from inside car", "through windshield"
- "driver's point of view", "in-car camera perspective"
- Wide angle lens, hood of car slightly visible at bottom

## Tips for Generation

1. **Add dashcam prefix**: For prompts 6-50, add this prefix:
   > `"Dashcam footage, driver's POV through windshield, "`
   
2. **Generate 2-3 variations** per prompt for diversity
3. **Resolution**: Request 1920x1080 or higher (dashcam typical resolution)
4. **Aspect ratio**: Landscape 16:9 (standard dashcam format)
5. **Quality**: Add "photorealistic", "4K", "high resolution"
6. **No signs**: Keep "no traffic signs visible" in prompts
7. **Dashboard hint**: Optionally add "car hood slightly visible at bottom"

### Example Enhancement for Prompt 6:
**Original**: `Colombo street during monsoon rain, wet asphalt road...`

**Enhanced**: `Dashcam footage, driver's POV through windshield, Colombo street during monsoon rain, wet asphalt road reflecting lights, rain on windshield, wipers visible, realistic, 4K`
